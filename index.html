How it works

From live SAP data to an approved action

Every assistant follows the same six steps. The agent does the first four. Your team owns the decision.

  1. ConnectHealth check and secure login to SAP before any number is reported.
  2. ReadTargeted queries on live SAP objects. No full-table scans.
  3. ClassifyYour business rules, thresholds and tolerances applied consistently.
  4. RecommendAn Action Ticket: finding, impact, fix and where to execute it.
  5. ApproveA supervisor approves in chat or in Microsoft Teams.
  6. Execute & verifyThe change is made in SAP and read back to confirm it.

Built for control

Your team stays in control

  • Read-only by default. Agents look and recommend. Nothing changes in SAP until your team approves it.
  • No invented numbers. Every metric comes from an SAP query in that session. Gaps are stated plainly.
  • Severity you can act on. Watch, Advise, Recommend and Escalate tiers match how a shift is run.
  • Fits your tools. Action Tickets post to Microsoft Teams and become Planner tasks automatically.
  • One click into SAP. Findings link straight to the Fiori app or transaction to fix them.
  • Your rules, your thresholds. SLAs, tolerances and capacity limits are configurable per site.

FAQ

Questions about AI agents for SAP

More answers on our FAQ page, or see our SAP Business AI and Enterprise AI services.

What are Körber Stellium AI agents?

They are AI assistants that work inside your SAP execution systems. Each one reads live data from SAP EWM, SAP TM or SAP Digital Manufacturing, applies your business rules, and hands your team a ranked recommendation or a ready-to-approve action.

Which SAP systems do the agents connect to?

SAP Extended Warehouse Management (EWM), SAP Transportation Management (TM) and SAP Digital Manufacturing (DM), on S/4HANA and SAP cloud tenants, through the Körber Stellium SAP Connector and SAP DM APIs.

How do recommendations reach my team?

As Action Tickets with the finding, the impact, the recommended step and the SAP transaction or Fiori app to use. Tickets can be posted to a Microsoft Teams channel, where a flow creates Planner tasks automatically.

How long does it take to get started?

A typical pilot starts with one assistant, one site and read-only access. Talk to us about an assessment of your EWM, TM or DM landscape to scope it.
