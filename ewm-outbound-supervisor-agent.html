The challenge

Outbound problems show up late, when the truck is already at the dock

Supervisors juggle wave monitors, task queues and delivery lists across several screens. A bottleneck that is easy to fix at 60% of the shift turns into a missed carrier at 95%.

The Warehouse Outbound Supervisor assistant watches the whole outbound flow in SAP EWM. It sees problems early, ranks them by severity and recommends a specific fix, naming the wave, area or resource and the EWM app to use.

Business rules

Severity tiers that match how a shift is run

Every finding gets one of four tiers. Recommend and Escalate findings always become an Action Ticket. Thresholds are regional-DC defaults and adopt your site's SLAs.

Tier Meaning What the agent does
Watch Normal variance Notes it and keeps monitoring
Advise Worth attention this shift Surfaces it with a specific fix
Recommend Needs a decision soon Raises an Action Ticket
Escalate Time-critical or customer-impacting Surfaces it immediately with an Action Ticket

Guardrails

Safe to run on production SAP

  • Read-only. No exceptions. The agent identifies and recommends. It never releases a wave, reassigns a task or touches a delivery.
  • Every number traced. Each metric comes from a live query in that session. Failed queries are reported, never filled in.
  • Unpopulated fields are called out. A blank field means missing data, not an idle team or an empty warehouse.
  • Customer data stays minimal. Names and addresses are pulled only for delayed or exception rows.
  • No guesswork on status. Statuses are reported as SAP shows them, never assumed.
  • Efficient by design. A full sweep is about eight targeted queries, so it can run every 15 to 30 minutes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does the SAP EWM outbound AI agent monitor?

Wave progress against cutoff, open task aging and bin congestion, labor pace against target by area, and outbound delivery exceptions such as delayed shipments and short picks.

Can the agent release or change waves in SAP EWM?

No. It is read-only. It recommends a specific change and names the EWM app or transaction, and your supervisor makes the change.

How often does it run?

On demand when a supervisor asks, and on a standing 15 to 30 minute cadence during active wave execution, with a full handover at shift change.

Where do the Action Tickets go?

They appear in the handover report and can be posted to a Microsoft Teams channel, where a Power Automate flow turns them into Planner tasks.
