The challenge

Freight audit teams spend their week on disputes that follow the same rules

Most carrier invoice disputes in SAP TM fall into a few patterns: a clean match, a small difference SAP already accepted within tolerance, or an overcharge that needs a conversation with the carrier.

The Carrier Invoice Dispute assistant applies those patterns consistently. It reads dispute cases, charge lines and tolerance logs in SAP TM, checks fuel surcharges against public diesel prices, and keeps every carrier conversation to a short, fixed format.

Business rules

Three business cases, applied the same way every time

The agent looks at how SAP TM rated the dispute and how large the difference is. The 5% threshold is configurable.

Case Condition Agent action
Case 1 Invoice matches the FSD exactly, no dispute case Reports it as clean
Case 2 Mismatch, but SAP already accepted it within tolerance Approves the dispute after your confirmation
Case 3 New dispute, difference above 5% Counter-mails the carrier, adds a fuel check if needed, escalates after round 2
Gap New dispute, difference of 5% or less Flags it for human review, no email
Skip A person or the carrier already acted, or the case is canceled Reports only

Guardrails

Safe to run on production SAP

  • Asks before acting. Every SAP approval and every carrier email is confirmed by a person in a live session.
  • Recipient read fresh from SAP. The carrier email comes from the business partner record at send time. Never guessed or reused.
  • Two rounds, then people. The agent never sends a third mail. It escalates with the full history.
  • Checks before every send. If the carrier has already responded in SAP, the mail is not sent.
  • Verifies every write. After an approval, the agent re-reads SAP to confirm the status changed.
  • Transparent evidence. Fuel prices come from public U.S. EIA data, cited in the mail.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a freight invoice dispute in SAP TM?

When a carrier invoice differs from the Freight Settlement Document, SAP TM opens a dispute case showing the overall difference and the difference per charge. The agent reads both to decide the next step.

Does the agent approve disputes automatically?

It prepares approvals for disputes SAP already passed in its tolerance check and asks you to confirm before it approves them in the Fiori app. Scheduled runs stop before any write unless you have given standing approval.

How does the fuel surcharge check work?

It takes the latest weekly U.S. on-highway diesel average from the EIA and scales the FSD fuel amount by the change in diesel price. A flat or falling diesel price never justifies an increase.

What happens if a carrier does not respond?

The agent sends at most two rounds, at least two business days apart, then escalates the case to your freight settlement team.
